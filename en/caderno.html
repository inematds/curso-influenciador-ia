Worksheets to prepare and follow through

Original worksheets for the fictional adult character Lia Lume. Copy them into your notes. No social media account was operated and no campaign was run to create this material.

Example character DNA and scene prompt

Lia Lume — fictional adult character, 32 years old.
Medium-brown skin; short black hair with a blue-green streak; dark eyes.
Plum jacket; cream shirt and pants; gold semicircle brooch.
3D illustration style with paper and clay texture.
Keep the face, hair, clothing, brooch, and style consistent.
Vary the pose, object, and a coherent setting.
Scene: keep the character from the reference and show her holding a notebook.
Simple background, soft light, hands and face clearly visible. No text in the image.

Write an original identity for your project. The description does not guarantee a result; compare each file with the approved reference.

Four-page carousel script

  1. Question: Where do you keep your pencils? Virtual character created with AI.
  2. Option: An illustrated arrangement for bringing the supplies together.
  3. Criterion: Check the space, condition, and stability of the real object.
  4. Invitation: Open the free worksheet and plan one possible change.

Use readable text in the editor; do not rely on letters generated inside the image. The four pages make up one editorial post.

Separate disclosures

Editorial post: virtual character created with AI. Illustrative scene.
Offer study: virtual character created with AI. Fictional offer study, no commercial partnership.
Real relationship: disclose AI + advertising and the actual relationship, as required by context and platform rules.
Real paid referral: disclose that a commission may be earned, in addition to any applicable advertising disclosure.

The editorial rule for this challenge identifies the character in every post. Also check the network’s requirements. An AI notice does not replace a commercial disclosure. The 2026 CONAR guide covers advertising and responsibility; it does not create a new universal requirement to label all AI.

Five groups of signals

  1. Exposure: views or reach; do not treat different definitions as equivalent.
  2. Watch time: viewing time or retention, when available.
  3. Usefulness: saves or shares, depending on the goal.
  4. Interest: clicks or relevant questions, using a stated criterion.
  5. Outcome: qualified conversations or actual transactions; may not apply to a study.

Three offers to study

Service

A short product video with its scope, revisions, deadline, and usage defined. Say it is available only if you can produce it and meet the terms.

Your own product

An existing, reviewed set of original planning templates. Do not resell someone else’s materials or INEMA’s free courses as your own.

Paid referral

A relevant product in a program you actually belong to. Check the rules, rights, fit, and commission disclosure. This course does not imply an INEMA affiliate relationship.

Close with evidence

21 editorial posts + 1 additional offer = 22 posts. Keep the files and URLs. Complete three weekly reviews and the final scheduled check-in after the last post. Record any daily gap as an extended calendar without changing the actual dates.
