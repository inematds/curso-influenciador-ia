Before you begin

Know how to generate images and edit simple pieces. If needed, revisit Kling, CapCut, and Social Media. For budgeting and offers, use Monetization. External tools may require an account and credits; check costs before generating.

Set up the account and identity, and prepare at least three reviewed pieces before day 1. The challenge requires 21 editorial posts and one additional offer post: 22 in total. Include video and a carousel in the series. The offer can be a clearly labeled fictional study, with no charge or invented partnership.

The student publishes manually. Tracking only stores local records and lets you export a copy. It doesn’t send messages, publish or schedule posts, or check external accounts. Dates, files, and pending items must be accurate.

The course consists of reading and practice, with no recorded video lessons. Marking lessons as read does not prove 21 days of execution. The final metrics check may happen after day 21. There is no guarantee of followers, clients, or income.
