Separate AI disclosure from advertising disclosure

You can prepare two clear disclosures for different situations.

Saying an image uses AI does not, by itself, tell people whether payment, a commission, or another commercial relationship exists.

In 1 minute

Use a clear introduction in the profile and every post: virtual character created with AI.

This is an editorial rule for the challenge, including for stylized images.

Also check the AI disclosure controls on your chosen social network.

A caption does not replace a disclosure required by the platform.

TikTok requires disclosure for realistic synthetic content.

YouTube requires disclosure for realistic scenes that are generated or significantly altered.

Meta uses AI disclosures according to its policies and available signals.

Control names and locations may change; check official sources before publishing.

For a real relationship, describe it accurately and use the disclosures required by the network.

The CONAR guide does not create a new universal requirement to label all AI.

Our editorial rule and platform rules are separate layers.
